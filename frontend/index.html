Trakerz is a lightweight Statement of Work, revenue and staffing tracker built for consulting and services teams. It runs entirely on your own machine — no cloud account, no subscription, no data leaving your laptop — while still giving you the dashboards and forecasts you'd expect from a hosted SaaS tool.

No sign-up. No servers to manage. Your data stays in a single local SQLite file.

Reports — KPIs, resource and status breakdowns at a glance

What is Trakerz?

Trakerz keeps everything a services or consulting team needs to track a book of client work in one connected app: the Statements of Work themselves, the revenue and staffing tracked against each one through Best Estimates, and the customer and master data everything else is built on. Instead of juggling spreadsheets that fall out of sync, every number on Reports is computed live from the same records you manage in Statement of Work, Best Estimates and Configuration — so the totals always agree.

Features

Reports & analytics

Live KPI tiles, SOW status and billing-model breakdowns, and resource distribution — all filterable by customer.

Statement of Work Management

Track every SOW's customer, dates, total Statement of Work value, billing model, status, milestones and linked documents in one sortable, searchable table.

Best Estimates

Log and review monthly revenue projections — by Statement of Work for Managed Services, or by employee assignment for Time and Material, with T&M projections calculated automatically from billing hours, holidays and leave.

Staffing & resources

Assign employees to a SOW with their band, employee type and location from Best Estimates > Time and Material, and see the breakdown by location, band and type on Reports.

Customer & master data

Maintain your customers — including delivery and sales contacts — plus billing hours, holiday calendar and resource leave under Configuration, and locations, billing models, operating models, SOW statuses, employee types, employee bands, opportunity types, revenue types and practices under Global Settings.

Alerts that find you

A dismissible banner flags SOWs that are overdue, expiring within 30 days, or over budget, so nothing slips through unnoticed.

Export to Excel

One-click export from the Statement of Work, Best Estimates and Customer tables whenever you need the numbers outside the app.

Selectable color themes

Pick from six palettes in the topbar — including the navigation bar's own color — with your choice remembered across sessions so the app always opens the way you left it.

Getting started

Follow these steps in order the first time you set up Trakerz — each one builds on the last.

  1. Configure your master data

    In Global Settings, set up Locations, Billing Models, Operating Models, SOW Statuses, Employee Types, Bands, Opportunity Types, Revenue Types and Practices to match how your organization works.

  2. Add your customers

    Create a customer record for each client you deliver work for, under Configuration → Customer.

  3. Create a Statement of Work

    Add a new SOW under a customer with its dates, total Statement of Work value, billing model and status from the Statement of Work Management page.

  4. Add milestones and staff it

    Break the SOW into milestones from its detail page, then assign employees to it — with their band, type and location — from Best Estimates → Time and Material.

  5. Track monthly revenue

    For Managed Services SOWs, log monthly Projections from Best Estimates → Managed Services; Time and Material projections are calculated automatically from billing hours, holidays and leave.

  6. Watch it on Reports

    KPIs, SOW status, expiring SOWs and resource allocation update automatically as you go — nothing to refresh or recalculate by hand.
