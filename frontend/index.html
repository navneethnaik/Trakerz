Trakerz is a lightweight Statement of Work, revenue and staffing tracker built for consulting and services teams. It runs entirely on your own machine — no cloud account, no subscription, no data leaving your laptop — while still giving you the dashboards and forecasts you'd expect from a hosted SaaS tool.

No sign-up. No servers to manage. Your data stays in a single local SQLite file.

Dashboard — KPIs, revenue trend and status breakdowns at a glance

What is Trakerz?

Trakerz keeps everything a services or consulting team needs to track a book of client work in one connected app: the Statements of Work themselves, the revenue projected against each one month by month, and the people staffed to deliver the work. Instead of juggling spreadsheets that fall out of sync, every number on the Dashboard is computed live from the same records you manage in Statement of Work Management, Revenue Management and Staffing — so the totals always agree.

Features

Dashboard & analytics

Live KPI tiles, SOW status and billing-model breakdowns, and resource distribution — all filterable by customer.

Statement of Work Management

Track every SOW's customer, dates, total Statement of Work value, billing model, status, milestones and linked documents in one sortable, searchable table.

Revenue management

Log monthly Projections per SOW across the fiscal year, for Managed Services and Time and Material alike.

Staffing & resources

Assign employees to SOWs with their band, employee type and location, and see who is staffed where at a glance.

Customer & master data

Maintain your own customers, billing hours and holiday calendar under Customer Configuration, and locations, billing models, operating models, SOW statuses, employee types, bands and practices under Global Settings.

Alerts that find you

A dismissible banner flags SOWs that are overdue, expiring within 30 days, or over budget, so nothing slips through unnoticed.

Export to Excel

One-click export from the Statement of Work Management, Revenue and Staffing tables whenever you need the numbers outside the app.

Light & dark mode

A polished theme for either preference, remembered across sessions so the app always opens the way you left it.

Getting started

Follow these steps in order the first time you set up Trakerz — each one builds on the last.

  1. Configure your master data

    In Global Settings, set up Locations, Billing Models, Operating Models, SOW Statuses, Employee Types, Bands, Opportunity Types, Revenue Types and Practices to match how your organization works.

  2. Add your customers

    Create a customer record for each client you deliver work for, under Customer Configuration → Customer.

  3. Create a Statement of Work

    Add a new SOW under a customer with its dates, total Statement of Work value, billing model and status from the Statement of Work Management page.

  4. Add milestones and staff it

    Break the SOW into milestones, then assign employees to it from the Staffing page with their band, type and location.

  5. Track monthly revenue

    Log monthly Projections for each SOW, month by month, from Revenue Management.

  6. Watch it on the Dashboard

    KPIs, revenue trends, expiring SOWs and resource allocation update automatically as you go — nothing to refresh or recalculate by hand.
